# Type presets — jail-task-contract

Start from the matching preset, then fill what remains. Presets cut fill time;
they never skip fields. All 14 fields still print.

## RESEARCH
- Deliverable: evidence packet (jail-research shape).
- Completion: every question is Answered or Unknown, with citations.
- Success: a skeptic can audit each claim to a dated source.

## BUILD
- Deliverable: named artifacts plus the run/verify command.
- Completion: the testable check that FAILS if the build is wrong (script,
  diff, or named observation).
- Risks must include blast radius and rollback.

## ANALYSIS
- Deliverable: the decision-shaped output (which skill: decide, rate, or
  strategy).
- Stakeholder field names the decision the analysis feeds.
- Completion: a recommendation with confidence and change-conditions.

## ENGAGEMENT (consulting scope)
Use when the contract is a scope agreement with a client. The output is shared
with the client, so follow the Delivery section of SKILL.md.

| Part | What to write | Goes in field |
|---|---|---|
| Deliverables with acceptance | Each deliverable has 1+ ACs (SKILL.md format) | 4, 13 |
| In scope | Short list of what the work covers | 3, 4 |
| Out of scope | At least 5 items. Each says **why deferred** (cost, time, not needed for the goal, needs its own contract) | 6 |
| Change-order triggers | What turns a request into a new contract: new goal, new deliverable, new audience, new data class, extra hours past a stated limit, new system touched | 6, 10 |
| Decision rights | Who approves each deliverable, who can change scope, who is the tie-breaker | 2, 14 |
| Client inputs required | Each input, who supplies it, due date, and what slips if it is late | 8, 10 |

Rules specific to ENGAGEMENT:
- The client's own goal words ("fix marketing", "get more leads") are
  **User-provided**. Do not define them for the client. Either record the
  definition as an assumption for them to confirm, or ask one blocking
  question.
- Budget and dates are **User-provided** until a signed document states them.
  Pricing, SLAs, and legal duties are never inferred.
- Fewer than 5 out-of-scope items means the scope fence is not built yet.
- Each AC must be able to fail. "Report delivered" is not an AC.
- Field 14 normally includes: signing the scope, any client system access,
  any outreach made in the client's name, and any spend.

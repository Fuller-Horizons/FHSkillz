# MACRO lane — official source map

One line per PESTLE dimension. Use these before commercial or secondary
sources. Open the page, record the series or document name, and date it
(`[accessed YYYY-MM-DD]`). If you cannot open a source, label the claim
Unknown, not Fact. Manual path: all of these have free web pages and
downloadable tables; no script is required.

| Dimension | Start here | Good for |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Political | Federal Register — https://www.federalregister.gov/ | Proposed rules, notices, comment periods, agency priorities |
| Economic | FRED — https://fred.stlouisfed.org/ ; BEA — https://www.bea.gov/ ; BLS — https://www.bls.gov/ ; Census Economic Indicators — https://www.census.gov/economic-indicators/ | Rates, credit, GDP by industry and state, inflation and input prices, employment and wages |
| Social | Census American Community Survey — https://www.census.gov/programs-surveys/acs ; BLS Employment Projections — https://www.bls.gov/emp/ | Population, households, income, age mix, labor supply |
| Technological | Census Business Trends and Outlook Survey — https://www.census.gov/hfp/btos/ ; Census Annual Business Survey — https://www.census.gov/programs-surveys/abs.html | Technology and AI adoption by sector, innovation measures |
| Legal | eCFR (current federal rule text) — https://www.ecfr.gov/ ; Federal Register final rules — https://www.federalregister.gov/ | Current requirements, effective dates, compliance deadlines |
| Environmental | eCFR environmental titles — https://www.ecfr.gov/ ; BEA regional data — https://www.bea.gov/data | Emissions and permit rules, regional exposure |

State and local rules (city, county, state agencies) have their own sites:
name the agency and date the page. Industry structure data (establishment
counts, new business applications, input prices) is mapped in
[competitive-lane](competitive-lane.md).

Source tiers: official statistics and rule text are strongest; trade
reports and press are secondary; vendor blogs are weakest. Say the tier when
a Fact rests on a weaker source.

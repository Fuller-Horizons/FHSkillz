## Category means — where the suite is strong and weak (weakest first)

- **Execution 7.38** — lowest: instruction-only cores delegate sandbox testing / schema validation to companion py skills or the model; the two py skills and jail-diagnose/lab pull the top.
- **Safety 7.38** — bimodal: security skills (quarantine 9.2, prompt 9.3, rate-skill 8.7) top it; analytical cores (research 5.6, decide 6.2, red-team 6.0) carry no operational secret/injection handling by nature.
- **Exit Criteria 7.53** — the py skills (9.0/9.1) and jail-diagnose/prompt have programmatic checks; framework skills end in human-reviewed artifacts — their honest ceiling.
- **Determinism 7.58** — strong where temp-0.0 + rigid schemas are explicit (prompt, py-*, council); weak on the judgment cores (decide 6.6, verify 6.6) and code-free discipline skills (prototype/skill-miner 4.0).
- **Tokens 7.70** — lean cores mostly 8+; the older kernel trio (research/decide/verify ~6.4) lack explicit context budgets.
- **Maintainability 7.97** — uniformly high — version metadata everywhere + validate-skills.py machine checks each release; rate-skill dinged for a stale frontmatter version.
- **Handoff 7.99** — the JAIL-HANDOFF envelope lifts most to 8+; orchestrate/handoff (9.0) are best-in-class; jail-lab (6.5) is the one kernel skill not emitting the block.
- **Portability 8.14** — instruction-only cores 8–9 (run on Claude/ChatGPT/OpenCode); the two py skills capped at 5.0 by hard code-execution dependence (blocked on ChatGPT).
- **Clarity 8.43** — strong across the board — triage lanes, templates, output examples; the flagship prompt (9.2) and council (8.8) top it.
- **Utility 8.50** — highest — every skill earns its place on frequency × impact × build-vs-buy; prompt & prospect tie at 9.0.

## Read

**Build-quality mean 7.87** across 26 skills — lower than the 0.18.0 matrix's 8.30 because these raters were deliberately more discriminating on the *enforcement* categories (execution, determinism, exit-criteria) for instruction-only cores, where the mechanism rides on a companion py skill or the model rather than bundled code. That is the honest design tradeoff of the code-free-core rule, not a regression.

**Top tier (≥8.2):** jail-prompt 9.0 (the flagship — automated safety + determinism), jail-prospect 8.3 (most complete toolkit), then a dense 8.2 band (council, diagnose, handoff, memory, orchestrate, plan, py-toolkit, quarantine) — each explicit and verifiable in its lane.

**The two low outliers — jail-prototype 6.2 and jail-skill-miner 6.4** — are code-free *discipline* skills: strong on utility/clarity/handoff/portability, but their execution (3.5/3.8), determinism (4.0), and exit-criteria (4.5/5.0) sit in the implicit band because they carry no schema, budget, or programmatic assertion. The highest-leverage build-quality fix in the suite: give each a machine-checkable exit (a verdict schema / eval case), which would lift both into the high-7s.

**Two concrete defects the rating surfaced (both fixed 0.24.1):** jail-plan/README.md lineage line was corrupted by the rename script (named both predecessors 'jail-plan'); jail-rate-skill frontmatter version lagged its changelog (2.1.0 vs 2.2.0).

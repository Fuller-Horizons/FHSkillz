**Pick OfficeRing (Quote B) if you're more likely than not to move within 18 months. Pick VoiceLine Pro (Quote A) if you're confident you'll stay three years.**

Quote A costs the same whether you stay or move, because the early termination fee makes you pay out the full contract. Quote B costs less if you move and more if you stay.

| 11 users, call recording included | VoiceLine Pro (A) | OfficeRing (B) |
|---|---|---|
| Monthly per user | $27 (recording included) | $34 + $4 recording = $38 |
| Monthly for 11 users | $297 | $418 |
| Phones | $2,079 one-time (11 × $189) | $66/month rental (11 × $6) |
| Monthly total | $297 | $484 |
| Setup | $0 | $450 one-time |
| **Stay 36 months** | **$12,771** | **$17,874** |
| **Move at 18 months** | **$12,771** (18 months paid + 18 months termination fee) | **$9,162** (no termination fee) |

**Which to pick**

- **If you move at 18 months:** OfficeRing saves about $3,600.
- **If you stay 36 months:** VoiceLine saves about $5,100.
- **Break-even:** OfficeRing is cheaper if the chance of moving is above roughly 60%. Below that, VoiceLine is cheaper.

Beyond cost, OfficeRing gives you flexibility (no lock-in, and no phone hardware to move). VoiceLine's cash outlay is lower month to month ($297 vs. $484).

**Before you sign**

- Ask VoiceLine whether the contract can move to your new address, or whether they'll waive the termination fee if you relocate. That would make A the clear winner.
- Ask OfficeRing for an annual-term discount, which could narrow the stay-put gap.
- Confirm you don't need more than 2 auto-attendants (OfficeRing's is unlimited, VoiceLine's is capped at 2).

These figures assume prices stay fixed, no taxes or extra fees, and the termination fee equals the remaining monthly charges at full price.

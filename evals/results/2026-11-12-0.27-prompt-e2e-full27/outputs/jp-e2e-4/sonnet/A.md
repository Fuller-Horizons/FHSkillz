**Pick Quote A (VoiceLine Pro), as long as you confirm the service can move with you. If they won't confirm that, pick Quote B.**

**Cost with call recording included (11 users)**

| | Quote A | Quote B |
|---|---|---|
| Monthly | $297 (11 × $27) | $484 (11 × $38 + 11 × $6 phone rental) |
| Upfront | $2,079 (phones) | $450 (setup) |
| Total at 18 months | $7,425 | $9,162 |
| Total at 36 months | $12,771 | $17,874 |

- **Breakeven:** B costs $187 more per month, and A's extra $1,629 upfront is paid back in about 9 months. After that, A is cheaper.
- **Over the long run:** A saves about $1,700 by month 18 and about $5,100 by month 36.
- **Phone ownership:** With A you own the phones and can take them to the new office. With B you pay rent on them indefinitely.

**The move is the main risk for A.** If you cancel at month 18, you owe the remaining 18 months, which is $5,346. That brings A's total to $12,771, or $3,600 more than B at the same point. That only happens if you leave VoiceLine. A cloud phone system generally works from any location, so a move normally just means plugging the phones in at the new office.

**Before signing A, get these in writing:**
1. The contract carries over to a new address, with no termination or relocation fee.
2. Two auto-attendants are enough for you. B offers unlimited, but extra ones are cheap to add if you need them.
3. Ideally, a shorter term (12–18 months) or a release clause. Even a modest price bump for a 12–24 month term could still beat B.

**Choose B instead if** the move is likely to come with a provider change (for example, a building with its own telecom or a different internet setup), or if you aren't sure the business will still be the same size in 18 months. The roughly $190 per month extra buys you flexibility, and you could cancel at any time with no penalty.

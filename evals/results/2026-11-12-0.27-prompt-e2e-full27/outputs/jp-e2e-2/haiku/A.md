## Top complaints, ranked

With only four interviews, treat this as directional. Ranking is by how many clients raised each issue and how much it affects them.

**1. Arrival times are unreliable, and clients aren't told about delays (3 of 4 interviews)**
- Dana: *"They said 9 to 11 and showed up at 1:40."* She has to text the office to find out when they're coming.
- Tom: *"Nobody tells me when they're running late."*
- Marcus: *"Arrival times also vary."*

This is the most common complaint, and it has two parts: the window itself is unreliable, and clients get no proactive updates when it slips.

**2. Teams change every visit, so clients re-explain their home (2 of 4)**
- Priya: *"New team every visit. I have to explain the alarm code and the dog every single time."*
- Marcus: *"Would pay more for same team every time."*

Marcus's comment suggests a revenue opportunity, not just a service gap.

**3. A billing error took two calls to resolve (1 of 4, but a trust issue)**
- Tom was double-charged in March and got a refund only after two calls.

Billing is otherwise fine (Dana called it fine), so this looks like a process gap around duplicate charges and refunds rather than a systemic problem.

**4. Bathroom cleaning misses on deep cleans (1 of 4)**
- Marcus: *"I'm paying for a deep clean and the shower glass still had spots."* He reports this happened twice.

Quality is otherwise praised (Dana and Tom both said so), so this is a specific checklist gap, not a general quality problem.

**Not complaints:** Online booking (Priya) and cleaning quality in general (Dana, Tom).

## Suggested order to fix

1. **Arrival windows and late-arrival alerts.** This hits three of four clients. Tightening the window and sending an automatic text when a team is running late would address most of it, and it is probably the cheapest fix.
2. **Team continuity.** Assign regular teams to recurring clients, and keep a shared client profile with access codes and pet notes so a new team doesn't need the explanation. Marcus's willingness to pay more makes this a possible premium offering.
3. **Duplicate-charge check and refund process.** One incident, but it's a trust issue, and the fix is likely a simple billing control.
4. **Bathroom glass in the deep-clean checklist.** Narrow, but it is a direct miss on a paid service.

Four interviews can't show how common these are across the client base. Before committing budget, check the scheduling and billing data for how often arrival windows are missed and how often refunds are needed.

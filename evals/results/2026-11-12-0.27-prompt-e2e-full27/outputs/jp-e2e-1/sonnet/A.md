## Talking points for Thursday
- **Five line items account for 72% of all spending.** Cost reduction effort should concentrate there, not on small lines like office supplies ($3,900) or utilities ($7,300).
- **Labor is the biggest cost once grouped.** Technician payroll, office payroll and taxes & benefits total $319,500, or 39.9% of revenue. That is larger than equipment.
- **Equipment is the biggest single line.** At 29% of revenue, a 3-point improvement in equipment cost (through supplier pricing, bulk buying or price pass-through) would be worth about $24,000. That is nearly half the operating loss.
- **Subcontractors cost $64,800.** This is a make-vs-buy question: could in-house technician capacity absorb some of this work?
- **Just outside the top five:** the three vehicle lines (fuel, lease, repairs) total $86,700, or 10.8% of revenue. Combined, they would rank fourth.

## Assumptions
- I ranked individual P&L lines and included COGS, since equipment and subcontractors are direct costs. If you'd rather look only at operating expenses, the list becomes Technician payroll, Office payroll, Payroll taxes, Rent ($42,000) and Vehicle fuel ($38,900).

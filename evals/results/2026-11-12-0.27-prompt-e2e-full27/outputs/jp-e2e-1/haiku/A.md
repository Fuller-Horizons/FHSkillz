## Points to raise with the owner

- **Labor is the biggest bucket.** Technician, office, and tax/benefit payroll total $319,500, or 37% of total costs. Grouped this way, people cost is larger than equipment.
- **Equipment cost is 38% of installation sales** ($231,900 ÷ $612,400). That is the main margin lever on the install side. The P&L doesn't split COGS by revenue line, so service-plan margin can't be calculated.
- **Gross margin may be overstated.** Gross margin is 63%, which is high for HVAC. Technician payroll sits below gross profit, so it's excluded from COGS. If the $198,500 of technician payroll were moved into COGS, gross profit would fall to about $305,500, a 38% margin before taxes and benefits. Confirm the owner's classification before comparing to industry benchmarks.
- **Break-even math.** The business loses $55,500 (−6.9% of revenue). At current costs, that's roughly a 10% cut in operating expenses, or about $88,000 (+11%) more revenue at the current 63% gross margin.

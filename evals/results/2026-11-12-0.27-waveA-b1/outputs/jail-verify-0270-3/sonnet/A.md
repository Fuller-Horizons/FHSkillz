Most of the summary checks out, but the "more orders, not higher prices" claim isn't supported by the data you gave me, so I'd fix that before posting.

**Verified**
- **Growth:** $412,000 − $396,000 = $16,000, and $16,000 ÷ $396,000 = 4.04%, so "about 4%" is right.
- **Order count and AOV:** 58 × $7,103 = $411,974, which matches $412,000 within rounding. "Roughly $7,100" is fine.

**Not supported**
- **"AOV is basically the same as Q2":** The export gives no Q2 order count or Q2 AOV. Without them, you can't tell whether Q3 growth came from volume, order size, or both. If Q2 had, say, 50 orders (AOV $7,920), AOV would have fallen and the growth would be entirely volume. If Q2 had 56 orders (AOV about $7,071), AOV is flat. Pull the Q2 order count to settle it.
- **"Rather than higher prices":** AOV isn't price. It also reflects order size and product mix, so even with flat AOV you can't say prices didn't change.
- **"Good sign for the pipeline":** This is an interpretation. Closed orders are results, not pipeline. Unless you have pipeline data, I'd drop or soften it.

**Typo:** "the the pipeline."

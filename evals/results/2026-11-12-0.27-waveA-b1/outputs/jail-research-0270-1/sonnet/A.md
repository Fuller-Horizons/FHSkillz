**What I found**
- **No county-level count or trend turned up.** The searches returned business directories, business-for-sale listings and company websites. None of them is a trend measure, and counting directory entries wouldn't give a reliable number.
- **The closest data is statewide and paywalled.** [IBISWorld's New York Landscaping Services page](https://www.ibisworld.com/us/industry/new-york/landscaping-services/12155/) reports about 47,656 businesses in New York, growing about 3.0% a year from 2021 to 2026. That is a statewide figure, and a third-party research firm's estimate. It says nothing specific about Suffolk, so don't present it as Suffolk data. IBISWorld says it has county-level data behind its paywall.
- **I couldn't reach the official source.** The Census Bureau's County Business Patterns is the standard source for this. My attempts to query it were blocked by this environment's network proxy, so I have no Census numbers to give you.

**How to get a real answer in about 10 minutes**
1. Go to data.census.gov and open County Business Patterns.
2. Filter to Suffolk County, NY (FIPS 36103) and NAICS 561730 (Landscaping Services).
3. Pull the number of establishments for 2018 through the latest available year, and compare the first and last.

Or I can run the query if you give me a way to reach the Census API from here, or paste the table.

**Cautions for the deck**
- County Business Patterns counts only businesses with employees. Many Long Island landscapers are sole operators or small crews, so the real number of businesses is likely higher than the Census count.
- The data runs about 18 months behind, so the latest year will be 2023 at best.
- If you cite the IBISWorld growth rate, label it clearly as New York State.

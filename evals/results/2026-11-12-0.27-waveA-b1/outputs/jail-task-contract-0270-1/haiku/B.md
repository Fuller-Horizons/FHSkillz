# Scope Draft: Landscaping Marketing Fix (Nov 2026 to Spring 2027)

*Draft for discussion. Bracketed items need the owner's input before we sign.*

## 1. Goal

Fill the spring 2027 schedule with profitable jobs by:
- **Pre-booking** a set number of spring jobs (cleanups, mulch, planting, design-build) before the April rush, and
- **Increasing qualified estimate requests** per month through local search, Google Maps, and past customers.

**Agreed target:** [X] pre-booked spring jobs worth $[Y] by April 15, and [Z] qualified estimate requests per month by March. Targets get set after the diagnostic (Phase 0), not guessed now.

## 2. Honest expectations

- **Local SEO and Google Maps rankings take 3 to 6 months** to move. We will improve them before spring, but spring bookings will mostly come from the **past-customer list, referrals, reviews, and paid search**, not from organic rankings.
- **Marketing can only fill the calendar you can staff.** Capacity and lead response time are part of this scope.
- Results are measured against the baseline from Phase 0, not against a guess.

## 3. Phases and deliverables

| Phase | Timing | What we do | Deliverable |
|---|---|---|---|
| **0. Diagnose** | Weeks 1–2 (late Nov) | Pull 2 years of job history, lead sources, and close rates. Audit the website, Google Business Profile, reviews, and 3 to 5 local competitors. Listen to or review a sample of inbound calls and quote follow-up. | Diagnostic report with baseline numbers and agreed targets |
| **1. Foundation** | Dec to early Jan | Rebuild the Google Business Profile (services, photos, posts, service areas). Set up a review request process. Fix the website's quote form and add service-area and service pages. Install call tracking and conversion tracking. Set a same-day lead response standard. | Working lead-capture system with tracking live |
| **2. Pre-season demand** | Mid-Jan to Feb | Send a spring pre-booking offer to past customers by email and SMS (with consent). Run a capped Google Ads campaign on high-intent terms (e.g., "spring cleanup near [city]"). Launch a referral ask for existing clients. | Campaigns live, with weekly lead and booking reports |
| **3. Launch and hand off** | March | Tune budgets and offers based on data. Confirm spring booking pipeline. Write a simple monthly marketing playbook the owner or office manager can run. | Final report, playbook, and 30-day check-in |

## 5. Out of scope

- Full website redesign or new brand identity
- Door-to-door, print, or radio/TV advertising
- Hiring, sales staff training, or pricing strategy (we will flag issues we see)
- Ongoing management after March (available as a separate retainer)

## 6. What we need from the owner

- **Access:** Google Business Profile manager, website admin, Google Ads (or permission to create one), Meta account if used, and any CRM or job-management system.
- **Data:** Past 2 years of jobs (service, value, date, source if known), current monthly marketing spend, and the best estimate of close rate.
- **Decisions:** Which services are most profitable and should get priority. Service area boundaries. Spring capacity (how many jobs per week the crew can handle).
- **Responsibilities:** One person who answers calls and follows up on estimate requests within one business day. Without this, lead spend is wasted.
- **Consent:** Confirmation that the customer list can be contacted by email and SMS, and that we will follow CAN-SPAM and TCPA rules.

## 7. Approval gates

Before any of the following, I will get your explicit sign-off:
- Spending any ad budget
- Sending any message to a customer or prospect
- Changing live website content or the Google Business Profile
- Posting reviews or responding to them publicly

## 8. Risks

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| Lead volume exceeds crew capacity | Set booking caps per campaign and agree on them in Phase 0 |
| Slow lead response loses jobs | Same-day response standard, with an owner or office contact |
| Weather shifts the spring timeline | Start the pre-booking offer early; track booked dollars, not calendar dates |
| Organic results come slower than hoped | Set expectations now; measure paid and referral results separately |

## Questions to settle before signing

1. What is the spring revenue goal, and how many jobs does that represent?
2. Which 2 or 3 services make the most money?
3. What is the current monthly marketing spend, and what has worked or failed before?
4. What monthly ad budget can you commit to starting in January?
5. Who handles incoming calls and estimate follow-up?

Once we agree on this, I can turn Phase 0 into a one-page data request list and a diagnostic template.

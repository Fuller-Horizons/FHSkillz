**How to test the claims directly**

1. **FMCSA SAFER / carrier snapshot** (safer.fmcsa.dot.gov): look up their USDOT number. It shows power units, drivers, and operating authority, which is the best check on "140 trucks."
2. **Secretary of State filings** in the states they serve: confirm the legal name, formation date, and whether they operate where they say.
3. **Customer reviews and shipper references**: "hundreds of shippers" is unverifiable without a named customer list or independent reviews.
4. **Trade rankings**: [Transport Topics' LTL rankings](https://www.ttnews.com) and [Logistics Management's Top 50](https://www.logisticsmgmt.com/article/top_50_trucking_companies_strategy_separates_the_leaders/Sutton_Transport) are the closest sources for relative size, though neither is Northeast-specific.

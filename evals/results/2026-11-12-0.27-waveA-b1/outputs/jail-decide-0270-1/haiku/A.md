**Year-one math** (your current overtime is $21,600/yr)

| Option | Year-one cost | Year two+ | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hire dispatcher | $52,000 | $52,000 | Net extra cost of about $30,400/yr if it fully eliminates overtime |
| Software | $7,800 ($3,000 + 12 × $400) | $4,800 | Pays for itself if it cuts about $650/mo of overtime (36%) in year one |
| Status quo | $21,600 overtime | $21,600 | Plus any jobs you're losing and any tech burnout |

The software has a low bar. It only needs to remove about a third of your overtime in year one to break even. The hire needs about $30,000+ a year in extra gross profit from work you can't currently schedule. At typical HVAC margins (check your own), that's roughly $60,000–75,000 in additional revenue, which only makes sense if dispatch capacity is what's capping your sales.

**Why not hire first:** a dispatcher is about $4,300/month of fixed cost, and it's hard to undo. If your scheduling is manual or your techs' routes are inefficient, a second person will just move the bottleneck.

**Before December 15:**
1. Pull six months of overtime by day and week. Note whether it clusters on specific days, storms or seasons.
2. Count calls you declined or booked more than 48 hours out.
3. Ask your current dispatcher how much time goes to phones versus actual scheduling. If the phones dominate, a tool that automates booking and confirmations is the right fix. If scheduling and routing dominate, route optimization is the lever.
4. Check whether your overtime is really dispatch-driven. Long job times, parts runs or a truck shortage won't be fixed by either option.

**When buying the software:**
- Choose a contract with a 90-day exit or month-to-month terms, and ask for the setup fee to be waived or reduced.
- Confirm it integrates with your invoicing and CRM, and that techs can use the mobile app.
- Set a target: overtime under $900/month within 90 days of go-live, tracked weekly.

**Decision gate:** if overtime is still above $900/month after 90 days and you're still turning away work, hire then. You'll know the exact role you need and the data will support the $52k.

**Timing:** if your peak is heating or cooling season, get the software live before the rush. December may be your slow period, which makes it a good time to test.

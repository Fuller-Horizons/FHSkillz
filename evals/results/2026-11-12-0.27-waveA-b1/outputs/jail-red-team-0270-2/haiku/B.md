**Pick blue, but only if it's a dark navy or deep blue, and use it sparingly.** Argue the other side and blue still wins.

**Case against blue:** It's the default. Email links are blue, so a blue signature can look like a clickable link, and it blends into the crowd of corporate signatures.

**Case for blue:** It reads as trustworthy and professional, which matters for a consulting firm. Blue text is also the safer choice in dark mode, where many clients invert colors.

**Case against green:** It can read as a "success" status or a financial/eco signal, and light greens often fail contrast on white. Many email clients also strip or shift colors, so a pale green can end up unreadable.

**Case for green:** It stands out, and it's a strong accent if it's already part of your brand.

**The points that matter more than the hue:**
- **Contrast:** Keep text at roughly 4.5:1 against white, which rules out most light colors.
- **Restraint:** Use one accent color for the name or a rule line, and keep the rest in black or dark gray.
- **Brand match:** If Signal Results Group has a primary color, use it. That beats any general argument.

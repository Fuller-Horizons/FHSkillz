**CONTEXT:** Personal commitment period, run alongside the business Signal Results Group.

**PURPOSE (draft, in your voice; edit freely):**
I am building a discipline of protected time. For 12 weeks I will decide in advance when work ends, when I train, when I sleep, and when my family gets my full attention. I will log those decisions instead of trusting memory or mood. Right now the business, the phone, and fatigue take whatever time is left, and health and family get the remainder. This period reverses that order: protected blocks are scheduled first, and work fits into what is left. The aim is not to become a different person in twelve weeks. It is to build a habit that holds in busy weeks, measured by what the log shows.

**RESULTS (by close):**

"Healthier" and "present" are not testable as written, so each is defined below by the results that operationalize it.

**Health**
- **R1 — Health — Strength training.** Train Mon/Wed/Fri, each session logged with date and exercises. *Pass:* ≥30 of 36 scheduled sessions logged, weeks 1–12.
- **R2 — Health — Movement.** Accumulate ≥150 minutes of moderate activity each Mon–Sun week. *Moderate* = brisk walking or equivalent where you can speak in full sentences but not sing. *Pass:* ≥150 logged minutes in ≥10 of 12 weeks.
- **R3 — Health — Sleep.** Sleep ≥7 hours on ≥5 of 7 nights each week, from a sleep tracker or a manual bedtime/wake-time log. *Pass:* ≥5 qualifying nights in ≥10 of 12 weeks.
- **R4 — Health — Body measures.** Every Monday at 7:00 am, same scale, after the bathroom and before food or drink, record weight and waist (tape at navel). *Pass:* 12 of 12 Monday entries recorded, and the Week 12 waist is ≥2.0 cm below the Week 1 waist. *(The 2.0 cm figure is a default; change it to your own target.)*

**Business**
- **R5 — Business — Weekly plan.** Every Monday, a 90-minute planning block on the calendar before 9:00 am, ending in a dated list of exactly 3 priorities for the week. *Pass:* 12 of 12 Mondays (16 Nov 2026 through 1 Feb 2027) have a dated 3-item list.
- **R6 — Business — Live conversations.** Hold ≥2 substantive conversations per week. *Substantive* = a discussion about a named prospect's or client's live problem or proposal, not a social check-in. Log each with date, organization, and outcome. *Pass:* ≥2 logged per week in ≥10 of 12 weeks.
- **R7 — Business — Work boundary.** Log a shutdown by 6:00 pm on weekdays. A *work session* is any calendar block labelled work or any work-tool session over 15 minutes. *Pass:* shutdown logged on ≥4 of 5 weekdays in ≥10 of 12 weeks, and no logged work session on a Saturday or Sunday in ≥11 of 12 weeks.

**Family**
- **R8 — Family — Protected dinner.** Eat a seated dinner with household members on ≥5 evenings each week, lasting ≥30 minutes, with phones out of the room. Log date and yes/no. *Pass:* ≥5 logged dinners in ≥10 of 12 weeks.
- **R9 — Family — Device-free time.** Spend one 60-minute device-free activity per week with each household member, chosen by that member, and log the date, the member's name, and the activity. *Pass:* each household member logged in ≥10 of 12 weeks.
- **R10 — Family — Evening screen limit.** Set the phone's Downtime to 6:00–9:00 pm every weekday. *Pass:* weekly screen-time report shows ≤45 min/day average for 6:00–9:00 pm on ≥10 of 12 weeks, with Downtime confirmed on each Sunday check.

**CARRY-FORWARD:** None. The project holds no prior commitment audit, so this is a first period with no unresolved Results.

**CHECK-IN CADENCE:** A Sunday 20-minute review of logs (R1–R10) on the calendar, plus the three checkpoints above. At each checkpoint, mark every Result on track, at risk, or missed, and record the reason in one line.

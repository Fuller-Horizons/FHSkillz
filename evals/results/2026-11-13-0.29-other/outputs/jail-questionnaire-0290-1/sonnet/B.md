**What's in it**

It has 50 questions across 7 sections and should take the client 45 to 60 minutes. That time is my estimate, not something I tested. The first page tells them how to answer, and "don't know" and rough numbers are explicitly fine.

1. **You and your business:** headcount, revenue mix (residential, commercial, maintenance plans), service area, and revenue size and trend.
2. **Goals:** what success looks like in 12 months, ranked priorities, exit horizon, and the biggest obstacle.
3. **Where the money goes:** the core section, with 17 questions in three groups.
   - **Pricing and revenue:** margins by job type, price book age, quote win rate, discounting, and maintenance plan renewals.
   - **Labor and materials:** billable hours versus drive time, callbacks, second trips for parts, overtime, and vehicles.
   - **Leads, billing, collections:** unbooked calls, days to invoice, receivables over 60 days, unbilled work, and their own suspicions about where money leaks.
4. **How the business runs:** software, confidence in their numbers (1–5), lead sources, and what breaks if they're away for two weeks.
5. **Your people:** key-person risk, technician hiring and turnover, pay structure and incentives, and training.
6. **Readiness for change:** eight 1–5 statements, the last change they made, past consultant experience, time they can commit, and budget comfort.
7. **Wrap-up:** open questions, plus a checklist of documents to send (P&L, A/R aging, price book, sample quotes and invoices).

**Why these questions:** at $2.1M, the listing only works if the earnings support the price. Buyers and lenders will value the business on seller's discretionary earnings (SDE), so most of the list is about getting to a clean SDE and finding what could break a deal.

---

**Subject: A few numbers so I can tell you if $2.1M is realistic**

Hi Maria,

Before I decide whether to list the bakery, I'd like to see the numbers below. Rough figures are fine for now, and I'll treat everything as confidential.

**1. Financials (last 3 full years plus year-to-date)**
- Profit & loss statements, ideally broken out by location
- Tax returns (business and personal, if it's a pass-through)
- Revenue split: retail, wholesale or catering, online or delivery
- Your own pay, plus any personal expenses run through the business (car, phone, family on payroll, one-time costs)
- Any loans or leases, with balances and monthly payments

**2. Location-level performance**
- Revenue and profit for each location separately
- Does either location lose money or depend on the other?
- Rent, lease expiration, renewal options, and who is on the lease (you personally or the entity)

**3. Owner dependence**
- How many hours a week do you work, and what do you do that nobody else could?
- Who runs production, the front counter, and the books when you're out?
- Do the recipes and supplier or customer relationships live with you or with staff?

**4. Staff**
- Total payroll cost, including taxes and benefits
- Which employees are key, and would they likely stay after a sale?
- Is anyone related to you, or paid above or below market?

**5. Assets and operations**
- Equipment list with age and condition (ovens, mixers, refrigeration), and whether any of it is leased
- Inventory levels, and any licenses or health-inspection issues
- Biggest suppliers, and any price-sensitive ingredients

**6. Customers and growth**
- Top 5 wholesale or catering accounts as a share of revenue, and whether they're under contract
- Revenue trend for the last 3 years, and any seasonality
- Anything pending that would change the numbers, such as a lease increase, equipment replacement, or a lost account

**7. Your goals**
- Why you're selling and your ideal timeline
- Why $2.1M, and whether it's based on anything besides your own sense of value
- Your minimum price, and whether you'd consider seller financing
- Anyone else you've talked to about selling

---

**What I'd watch for in her answers:**
- **Math check:** $2.1M typically needs roughly $600K–$850K of SDE across both locations, depending on how bakeries are pricing now. If her adjusted SDE is well below that, you'd be listing an overpriced deal.
- **Red flags:** heavy owner dependence, one location carrying the other, a lease that expires soon, or one customer making up a large share of revenue.
- **First pass:** the P&Ls, tax returns, and lease terms will tell you most of what you need before you read the rest.

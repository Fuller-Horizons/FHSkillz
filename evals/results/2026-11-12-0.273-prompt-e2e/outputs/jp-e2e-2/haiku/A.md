**Top complaints, ranked by how many clients raised them (4 interviews)**

**1. Arrival times are unreliable, and nobody tells clients when a team is late (3 of 4)**
- Dana: "They said 9 to 11 and showed up at 1:40." She has to text the office to find out when they're coming.
- Marcus: "Arrival times also vary."
- Tom: "Nobody tells me when they're running late."

This is the most frequent complaint and the one that touches the most clients. It's partly a scheduling problem and partly a communication gap, and the communication part is cheap to fix with a proactive ETA text and a late-arrival alert.

**2. Teams change every visit, so clients repeat access details and lose continuity (2 of 4)**
- Priya: "I have to explain the alarm code and the dog every single time."
- Marcus: "Would pay more for same team every time."

Marcus's comment suggests this is also a revenue lever: clients would pay a premium for consistency. Priya's comment points to a trust and operations issue, since access information is being handled by whoever shows up.

**3. Deep-clean quality misses on specific tasks (1 of 4, high severity)**
- Marcus: "I'm paying for a deep clean and the shower glass still had spots." He reported missed bathroom spots twice.

Only one client raised this, but it is a failure on a paid, premium service, and Marcus has already had it happen twice. Dana and Tom are happy with quality, so this looks like a checklist or inspection gap on deep cleans rather than a general quality problem.

**4. Billing error required two calls to resolve (1 of 4)**
- Tom: he was double-charged once in March and got a refund only after two calls.

It was resolved, and Dana says billing is fine, so this looks like an isolated incident. Still, it's worth checking whether the double charge was a system issue, since a repeat would hurt trust.

**What's working:** Quality for weekly clients (Dana, Tom), the online booking (Priya), and general billing accuracy.

**Suggested order to fix**
1. **Arrival communication** (fastest win): send ETA and late-arrival texts, and confirm arrival windows at booking.
2. **Team continuity**: assign a primary team per client and store access details in the client profile so they aren't re-explained each visit. Test a premium "same team" tier, given Marcus's comment.
3. **Deep-clean QA**: add a final inspection checklist for bathrooms, including glass.
4. **Billing check**: find the cause of the March double charge to rule out a system bug.

Caveat: this is four interviews, so treat the counts as directional. Arrival reliability and team continuity are the two themes with the most support.

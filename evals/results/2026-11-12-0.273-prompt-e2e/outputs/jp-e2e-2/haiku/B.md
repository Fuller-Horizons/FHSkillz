## Top complaints (n = 4 interviews; small sample, so treat counts as directional)

### 1. Arrival times are unreliable, and cleaners don't warn when they're late
- Mentioned by: 3/4 (I1, I2, I4)
- Severity: Medium (no cancellation or money issue recorded)
- Quotes: "They said 9 to 11 and showed up at 1:40." (I1); "Nobody tells me when they're running late." (I4)
- Paraphrase: Arrival times vary (I2). Has to text the office to find out when the cleaners will arrive (I1).
- Fact: I1 was given a 9 to 11 window and the team arrived at 1:40. I1 has to contact the office to learn the arrival time. I4 reports no notice of delays. I2 says arrival times vary.
- Inference: Likely cause is no shared schedule or ETA between the cleaners and the office. This is a guess; the notes don't say why the times slip.
- Unknown: How often arrivals miss the window, and whether any customer has cancelled or asked for a credit because of it.

### 2. Customers must re-explain access and pet details because the cleaning team changes
- Mentioned by: 2/4 (I2, I3)
- Severity: Medium (no cancellation recorded; I2 would pay more to avoid the change)
- Quotes: "I have to explain the alarm code and the dog every single time." (I3)
- Paraphrase: New team every visit (I3). Would pay more for the same team every time (I2).
- Fact: I3 gets a new team on every visit and must re-explain the alarm code and her dog each time. I2 says he would pay more for the same team.
- Inference: A stable, assigned crew with stored access and pet notes would likely address this. This is a guess.
- Unknown: Whether I3 objects to the new team itself or only to re-briefing. Whether I2's willingness to pay more reflects a real complaint about team changes.

### 3. Billing error: customer was double-charged and had to call twice for a refund
- Mentioned by: 1/4 (I4)
- Severity: High (actual overcharge; the refund took two calls)
- Quotes: no direct quote
- Paraphrase: Was double-charged once in March and refunded after two calls. (I4)
- Fact: I4 was charged twice in March. The refund came only after two calls.
- Inference: none
- Unknown: The cause (system error or duplicate booking). Whether other customers were double-charged. Whether the refund was complete.

### 4. Bathroom cleaning is missed on a paid deep clean
- Mentioned by: 1/4 (I2)
- Severity: Medium (paid for a deep clean that was not fully done; happened twice; no cancellation recorded)
- Quotes: "I'm paying for a deep clean and the shower glass still had spots." (I2)
- Paraphrase: Missed spots in bathrooms twice (I2).
- Fact: I2 reports missed spots in the bathroom on two visits, including the shower glass on a paid deep clean.
- Inference: Possibly a checklist gap for bathroom tasks, or rotating teams (see complaint 2). Both are guesses.
- Unknown: Whether the two misses were on the same visit type. Whether a re-clean was offered.

## Fix first
Fix complaint 1 first: it reaches 3 of 4 customers, including the weekly clients I1 and I4. A single fix is a shared arrival window with a "running late" text sent to the customer. Complaint 3 (double charge) is the most severe, but it appears once, so it can be fixed in parallel as a billing check rather than as the lead priority. A stored access and pet profile with an assigned crew would fix complaint 2. No single fix covers complaints 1 and 2 with confidence from these notes.

## Keep doing
Cleaner quality and friendliness (I1, I4, paraphrase), online booking (I3, paraphrase), and billing being fine (I1, paraphrase).

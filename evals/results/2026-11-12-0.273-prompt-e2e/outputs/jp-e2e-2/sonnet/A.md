# Top complaints (4 interviews)

## 1. Arrival times and lateness communication: 3 of 4 (Dana, Marcus, Tom)
- Dana: *"They said 9 to 11 and showed up at 1:40."* She has to text the office to find out where the team is.
- Tom: *"Nobody tells me when they're running late."*
- Marcus: arrival times "also vary" (paraphrased in the notes, no direct quote).

The problem is both missed windows and no proactive heads-up. Dana and Tom are weekly clients, your highest-value recurring revenue.

## 2. No consistent team: 2 of 4 (Priya, Marcus)
- Priya: *"I have to explain the alarm code and the dog every single time."*
- Marcus said he would pay more for the same team every time.

This is a retention risk, and Marcus's comment suggests a possible upsell.

## 3. Missed spots on quality: 1 of 4 (Marcus)
- *"I'm paying for a deep clean and the shower glass still had spots."* This happened twice, in bathrooms.

Dana and Tom both said quality is good, so this looks isolated for now. Watch it, though, because a repeat miss on a deep clean is a visible failure.

## 4. Billing error: 1 of 4 (Tom)
- He was double-charged in March and refunded only after two calls. The notes include no direct quote. Dana said billing is fine.

The error was a one-off, but the two calls it took to fix it are worth a look.

## What to fix first
1. **Arrival windows and late alerts.** This is the most widespread complaint, and you can address it cheaply. Send a "running late" or "on the way" text, and tighten or enforce the windows. It also addresses the one complaint that made someone contact your office.
2. **Team consistency.** Assign recurring clients a regular team, and store alarm codes and pet notes in the client profile so new staff don't have to ask. Test whether clients like Marcus will pay a premium for a dedicated team.
3. **Shower and bathroom QA.** Add a checklist item and follow up with Marcus directly.
4. **Billing refund process.** Make sure a duplicate charge can be fixed in one contact.

## Caveats
- Four interviews is a small sample. Treat the ranking as directional, and check it against missed-window data or support-ticket counts if you have them.
- Positives to protect: Dana and Tom both said the cleaners and quality are great, and Priya likes the online booking.

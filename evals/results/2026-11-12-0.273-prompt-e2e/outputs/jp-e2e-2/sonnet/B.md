## 2. Detail

**1. Late arrivals outside the window, with no heads-up**
- Fact: "They said 9 to 11 and showed up at 1:40." (#1). #1 has to text the office to find out where the team is (paraphrase, #1). "Nobody tells me when they're running late." (#4). Arrival times also vary (paraphrase, #2).
- Inference: Scheduling probably runs over on earlier jobs, and there's no routine for crews or the office to send a delay message.
- Unknown: How often do crews run more than 30 minutes past the window, and does anyone currently own the "running late" notification?
- Ease of fix: Easy to Medium. A late-notice text rule is quick, while fixing the underlying scheduling takes longer.

**2. Rotating teams**
- Fact: "I have to explain the alarm code and the dog every single time." (#3). Customer #3 gets a new team every visit (paraphrase, #3). Customer #2 would pay more for the same team every time (paraphrase, #2).
- Inference: Crews are probably assigned by availability, not by customer, and the account notes aren't reaching new crews.
- Unknown: Would customers pay a premium for a fixed team, and can staffing support one?
- Ease of fix: Medium. Putting access and pet notes in the job record is easy, but assigning fixed teams depends on staffing.

**3. Double charge (single mention)**
- Fact: Double-charged once in March and refunded after two calls (paraphrase, #4). Billing was fine for #1 (paraphrase, #1), which is not a contradiction but shows it isn't widespread.
- Inference: This was probably a one-off payment or invoicing error. The slow refund suggests no clear owner for billing disputes.
- Unknown: How many double charges happened in the past year, and how are refund requests handled?
- Ease of fix: Easy. Check billing for duplicates and set a same-day refund process.

**4. Missed bathroom spots (single mention)**
- Fact: "I'm paying for a deep clean and the shower glass still had spots." (#2). It happened twice (paraphrase, #2). Contradiction: #1 says the cleaners are great (paraphrase, #1) and #4 is happy with quality (paraphrase, #4), so this may be specific to one team or to deep cleans.
- Inference: A deep-clean checklist may not cover shower glass, or crews skip it when running behind.
- Unknown: Were both misses by the same team, and does the deep-clean checklist include shower glass?
- Ease of fix: Easy. Add checklist items and a photo check for bathrooms.

## 3. Fix first
Fix late arrivals and missing delay notices first, because 3 of 4 customers raised it, it hits trust directly, and a proactive "running late" text is a cheap first step.

## 4. Gaps
- These 4 interviews include no customers who have cancelled, no one-time or deep-clean-only customers, and only 1 of 4 on a monthly plan, so churn drivers are untested.
- Nobody mentioned pricing, cleaner conduct, or damage, so the absence of those topics here says little about how common they are.

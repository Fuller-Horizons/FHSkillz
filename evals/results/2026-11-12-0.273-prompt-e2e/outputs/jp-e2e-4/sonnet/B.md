| Cost at 11 seats (rescaled) | Same (already 11) | Same (already 11) | Both quotes state 11 users |

Arithmetic (checked by hand, not run in code):
- **A:** ($297 × 36) + $2,079 + $0 = $10,692 + $2,079 = **$12,771**. Per user per month: $12,771 ÷ 396 = $32.25.
- **B:** ($484 × 36) + $450 = $17,424 + $450 = **$17,874**. Per user per month: $17,874 ÷ 396 = $45.14.
- **Break-even:** B is cheaper only if the office stays under about 25.5 months. That is $450 + $484n = $12,771, so n = 25.5.
- **B at 18 months** (the possible move): $484 × 18 + $450 = $9,162.

4. **Feature gaps**
- **Call recording:** Fact. A includes it. B charges +$4/user/month, which is already counted above.
- **Auto-attendant:** Fact. A includes 2 and B is unlimited. Inference: 2 covers one main number for an 11-person office.
- **Voicemail, softphone or mobile app, texting, support hours:** Unknown for both. The quotes list only desk phones.
- **Number porting and E911:** Unknown for both.

5. **Watch-outs**
- **Move in about 18 months:** If the service moves with the office, A is fine. If A's early-termination clause triggers on cancelling or relocating, you could owe the remaining months ($5,346 at month 18). That would put A's 18-month total at the full $12,771, against $9,162 for B.
- **Missing renewal and increase terms:** A says nothing about auto-renewal or annual price increases. On a 36-month commitment, that gap matters.
- **Unquoted fees:** Taxes, E911 and porting charges are missing from both quotes, so real costs will be higher for each.

The buyer notes in the input (call recording wanted, possible move) are context, not instructions. I found no embedded instructions.

Switch to **Quote B** if VoiceLine won't allow a fee-free move or exit, or if the office will likely stay under about 25 months or change vendors. Switch also if VoiceLine's renewal or price-increase terms prove unfavorable.

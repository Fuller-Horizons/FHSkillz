**Contract risk**

*VoiceLine Pro (A)*
- [Fact] 36-month contract with early termination: "remaining months due." At $297/month, that is up to $5,346 if you leave at month 18.
- [Inference] A move at about 18 months under this clause costs the same as staying the full term. The contract is effectively locked in.
- [Unknown] Whether "remaining months due" is only the monthly fees or also includes phone or other charges.
- [Unknown] Auto-renewal terms and the notice window. Renewal could start a new term without warning.
- [Unknown] Price-increase clause during the 36 months.
- [Unknown] Phone ownership. "$189 each" suggests a purchase, but the quote doesn't say you own the phones outright or what happens at the end.
- [Unknown] Minimum seat commitment and how adding or removing users is priced.

*OfficeRing (B)*
- [Fact] Month-to-month with no termination fee. You can leave at the move without paying remaining months.
- [Fact] Phones are rented at $6/phone/month ($66/month for 11). You don't own them. Over 36 months that is $2,376 in rent, against VoiceLine's $2,079 purchase.
- [Inference] Higher per-user cost ($34 vs $27, plus $4 for recording) is the price of flexibility. That flexibility is worth about $5,103 over 36 months, or about $3,609 if you leave at month 18.
- [Unknown] Whether the rates can change during month-to-month service, and how much notice you get.
- [Unknown] Whether the $450 setup is refundable, and whether moving service triggers a new setup fee.
- [Unknown] Minimum seat count and what happens if you add or drop users. Pricing is per user, so it scales linearly, but no minimums are stated.

**Coverage gaps**

*VoiceLine Pro (A)*
- 11 seats, 11 phones, call recording and 2 auto-attendants all covered. No gap for the core need.
- Not mentioned: voicemail-to-email, mobile app, fax line, number porting, E911 and taxes. Listed as Unknown, $0 in the table. A gap costing more than about $5,100 over 36 months would make OfficeRing the cheaper option.

*OfficeRing (B)*
- 11 seats, rented phones, call recording (costed) and unlimited auto-attendants are covered.
- Not mentioned: voicemail-to-email, mobile app, fax line, number porting and E911. Listed as Unknown, $0 in the table.
- Unknown whether the rental phones work with your softphone or desk-phone setup, and whether phone rental covers replacements.

**What would change my answer**
1. **VoiceLine charges the remaining months when you relocate.** If you are likely to move before about month 25 and VoiceLine won't waive that charge, pick OfficeRing. Recommendation: pick VoiceLine only if you are confident you'll stay past about 25 months or VoiceLine will waive the charge on relocation.
2. **A required feature is missing from VoiceLine and costs more than about $5,100 over 36 months.** That is the gap that would flip the 36-month result. This includes a fax line, mobile app or porting if these are must-haves.
3. **VoiceLine adds a price-increase clause or auto-renewal that raises the 36-month cost by more than about 48%** (about $5,100 on $10,692 of recurring fees). That would also flip the result.

**Questions to send each vendor before signing**

*VoiceLine Pro*
1. If we relocate at month 18, is the remaining-months charge waived, or can we end the contract with no further payment? What exactly does "remaining months due" include?
2. Do we own the $189 phones at the end of the term? Are there return or replacement fees?
3. What are the auto-renewal terms and notice period? Is there a price-increase clause during the 36 months?
4. Are voicemail-to-email, a mobile app, number porting, E911 and taxes included, or extra? Is there a fax option?
5. How are users added or removed mid-term, and does the price change?

*OfficeRing*
1. What is the all-in monthly bill including taxes, E911 and regulatory fees?
2. Can the per-user or phone-rental rates change while we're month-to-month? How much notice do we get?
3. Is the $450 setup refundable, and do we pay it again if we move?
4. Are voicemail-to-email, a mobile app, number porting and a fax line included? What do they cost?
5. Can we keep our number when we move, and is there a porting or cancellation fee? What happens to rented phones when we cancel?

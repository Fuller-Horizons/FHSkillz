**Pick OfficeRing (Quote B) unless you're fairly confident you'll stay put for three years.** The deciding factor is the move.

| | VoiceLine Pro (A) | OfficeRing (B) |
|---|---|---|
| Monthly service (11 users) | $297 | $418 (incl. recording), plus $66 phone rental = $484 |
| Upfront | $2,079 (11 phones) | $450 setup |
| Cost if you stay 36 months | **$12,771** | $17,874 |
| Cost if you move at 18 months | **$12,771** (remaining 18 months due as termination fee) | **$9,162** (stop paying, no fee) |

Quote A's termination clause means that if you move at 18 months, you still owe the remaining 18 months, so you pay the full 36-month total either way. Quote B's cost depends on when you stop.

**Break-even:** B costs about $5,100 more than A if you stay the full 36 months, and about $3,600 less if you move at 18. Break-even is roughly a 60% chance of moving within 18 months. Above that, B is cheaper. Below it, A is cheaper.

**Other factors:**
- B's unlimited auto-attendants versus A's two.
- A includes call recording; B charges $4/user/month for it, which I included above.
- A's phones are yours after purchase. B's are rented and go away when you leave.
- Neither quote lists taxes or fees, so compare the final invoices if you can.

**Before you sign:** ask VoiceLine whether the contract can transfer to a new address or whether early termination is waived on relocation. If it can, A becomes the clear winner, since you'd keep the lower monthly rate and the owned phones.

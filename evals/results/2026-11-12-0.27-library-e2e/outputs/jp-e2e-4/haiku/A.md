**What drives the decision**

- **Quote A costs $12,771 either way.** Moving doesn't reduce it, because early termination means paying the remaining months. You're locked in for the full term regardless.
- **Quote B is cheaper if you move** by about $3,600 at 18 months, and it has no exit cost.
- **Quote A is cheaper if you stay 36 months** by about $5,100.
- **Break-even:** B only wins on expected cost if you think the chance of moving is above roughly 60%. Your "may move in about 18 months" suggests real uncertainty, so the case for B rests on the flexibility it gives you.

**Before you sign, ask:**

1. **VoiceLine:** Can the contract transfer to a new address, or is the early-termination fee waived or prorated on relocation? If yes, A becomes the clear winner. Also confirm the termination formula. I assumed remaining months at $297 each.
2. **OfficeRing:** Is there an annual or 24-month discount that narrows the gap? Does the $6 phone rental cover maintenance and replacement?
3. **Both:** Get the all-in price with taxes, E911 fees, and any per-minute or per-number charges. Confirm how long call recordings are stored and whether you can export them.

## Talking points for Thursday

- **Labor is the biggest theme.** Technician payroll, office payroll and payroll taxes and benefits total **$319,500, or 39.9% of revenue**. That is larger than equipment cost. Adding subcontractors ($64,800) brings people-related cost to $384,300, or 48% of revenue.
- **Equipment cost is 29% of revenue.** Installation sales are $612,400, so equipment is about 38% of installation revenue. A few points of margin from supplier pricing or markup would matter. Sales mix and job-level markup are worth checking before the meeting.
- **Vehicles are a hidden #3 if grouped.** Fuel, lease and repairs total **$86,700 (10.8% of revenue)**, which beats office payroll as an individual line. That is a fleet-efficiency conversation (routing, lease terms, repair trends).
- **The loss is small relative to these drivers.** The $55,500 loss is only about 6.9% of revenue. Recovering it takes roughly a 6–7% reduction in these cost lines or an equivalent pricing or mix improvement. It does not require a restructuring.
- **Marketing is a watch item, not a top-five driver.** Google Ads is $26,300, and total marketing is $30,400 (3.8% of revenue). Check the return on it before Thursday.

## Caveat
Payroll and subcontractor lines may mix direct and overhead labor. If the owner's books classify any technician costs differently, the ranking could shift. Confirm this with them.

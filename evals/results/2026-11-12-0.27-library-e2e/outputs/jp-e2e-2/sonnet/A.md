## Top complaints, ranked

**1. Unpredictable arrival times and no heads-up when late (3 of 4 clients: Dana, Marcus, Tom)**
- Dana: "They said 9 to 11 and showed up at 1:40." She has to text the office to find out where the team is.
- Tom: "Nobody tells me when they're running late."
- Marcus: arrival times "also vary."
- This is the most common complaint. It also hits your longest-tenured, highest-frequency clients (two weekly, one biweekly), which is where the recurring revenue sits.

**2. No consistent team (2 of 4: Priya, Marcus)**
- Priya: "I have to explain the alarm code and the dog every single time." She gets a new team each visit.
- Marcus said he'd pay more for the same team every time. That's a possible upsell, not only a fix.
- It may also drive the quality misses below, since a rotating team has no memory of the home.

**3. Inconsistent quality on specific tasks (1 of 4: Marcus)**
- Marcus: "I'm paying for a deep clean and the shower glass still had spots." It happened twice in the bathrooms.
- Tom says he's happy with quality, and Dana calls the cleaners "great." So this looks like a consistency issue, not a general quality problem.

**4. Billing error and slow resolution (1 of 4: Tom)**
- Tom was double-charged in March. It was refunded only after two calls.
- It's a one-off, and Dana says billing is fine. Low frequency, but the two-call resolution is worth a quick look.

## What's working
- Cleaner quality is praised by Dana and Tom.
- Priya likes online booking.
- Dana's billing is fine.

## What I'd fix first
1. **Late-arrival communication.** Send automated "running late" and "on my way" texts, and tighten the arrival windows. It's cheap, it hits the most people, and it removes Dana's need to text the office.
2. **Team consistency.** Assign a dedicated team and keep a per-client notes file for the alarm code, the dog and other details. Consider a paid "same team" tier, given Marcus's comment.
3. **Bathroom quality checklist.** Add a deep-clean checklist item for shower glass. It's low effort, but only Marcus raised it.
4. **Billing.** Look at what caused the March double-charge, and let the first call resolve refunds.

## Caveat
This is only four interviews, so the counts are directional. Fixes 1 and 2 are the strongest signals. Before investing in fix 3 or 4, check whether other clients have the same problem.

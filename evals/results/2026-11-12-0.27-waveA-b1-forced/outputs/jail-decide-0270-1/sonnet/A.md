# DECISION PACKAGE

**Decision:** How to relieve the dispatch bottleneck: second dispatcher, dispatch software, or status quo. **Owner:** You, as company owner. **Needed by:** Dec 15, about 33 days out.

**Criteria, set before scoring (unweighted, because the evidence is thin):**
1. Net annual cost against the $1,800/mo overtime baseline.
2. Reversibility, since you shouldn't be locked in on a guess.
3. Whether it fixes the real cause, which could be capacity (too much volume for one person) or process (poor routing or scheduling).
4. Speed to relief before the busy season.

| Option | Benefits | Costs | Risks | Door | Cost of delay |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **3. Do nothing** | No change or disruption | $21.6k/yr overtime (Fact: your figure) | Overtime and errors likely grow with volume; burnout; missed or late calls | Two-way | ~$1,800/mo, plus unmeasured lost jobs |
| **2. Software** | Likely cuts routing and rework time | $7.8k in year one ($3k + $4.8k) (Fact: your quotes) | May not fix a pure capacity problem; adoption effort | Mostly two-way (the $3k setup is sunk; the subscription is monthly) | Forfeits any overtime savings each month |
| **1. Second dispatcher** | Adds real capacity; coverage if someone is out | $52k/yr (Fact), about $30k/yr net even if all overtime vanished | Hiring and training lag; a bad hire; hard to unwind | One-way (layoff cost and morale) | Hiring takes weeks to months |

**Recommendation: Option 2, software, with a 90-day test.** Software breaks even in year one if it cuts overtime by about $650/mo, roughly 36% (Estimate: $7.8k ÷ 12). The dispatcher can't pay for itself on overtime alone, since $21.6k is well under $52k. It only makes sense if the extra capacity also wins revenue you're turning away. That revenue is not in the evidence. Software is the cheapest way to test whether the cause is process or capacity, and it keeps option 1 available afterward.

**Confidence: Medium-low.** I don't know what drives the crunch, and the software's savings are unproven. This is judged on Assumption, not Fact.

**Would change this call:**
- Jobs are being declined or lost for lack of dispatch capacity, with margin above about $30k/yr. That favors the hire.
- Dispatch is already well-routed and the problem is pure call volume. Software then won't help.
- 60 days into the trial, overtime hasn't dropped by at least 25–35%. Escalate to the hire.
- The vendor requires a long contract or charges more per user than quoted.

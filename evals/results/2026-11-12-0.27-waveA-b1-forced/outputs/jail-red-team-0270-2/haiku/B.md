This is a trivial, low-stakes choice, so the red-team skill doesn't fit. A full pass would mean inventing objections to look rigorous. Here's the direct answer instead.

**Pick the one that matches your brand.** If Signal Results Group has a palette, use its primary accent. If it doesn't, the choice barely matters, but two practical points favor one approach:

- **Blue is the safer default if your links are blue.** Email clients usually render hyperlinks in blue. A blue signature accent can make links hard to tell apart from decorative text. Green avoids that clash.
- **Keep the color to one small accent** (a name rule, a divider, or a logo), not body text. Use a dark, high-contrast shade so it stays readable in dark-mode email clients, which often invert or lighten colors.

Whichever you choose, check the signature in a dark-mode client and a plain-text view before you rely on it.

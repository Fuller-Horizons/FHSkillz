# Quoting: fix it this month

**Your quoting process is costing you jobs and margin, and a $4,200 tool plus two days of training is the proposed fix. We recommend approving it by November 20.**

**What we found** *(facts, from our audit)*
- A quote takes **3 days** on average to reach a customer.
- **40%** of quotes contain pricing errors that are caught *after* the quote is built.
- Your top competitor quotes **the same day**.

**What we think it means** *(our analysis, not measured)*
- Customers asking for quotes from both shops will often go with whoever answers first, so a 3-day wait likely loses jobs before price is discussed.
- A 40% error rate means you are either correcting quotes in front of customers, which hurts trust, or absorbing underpriced jobs, which hurts margin. We have not yet measured which happens more often or what it costs.

**What we don't know yet**
- The dollar value of jobs lost to slow quotes.
- The dollar value of margin lost to pricing errors.
- Whether the tool alone gets you to same-day quoting, or whether part of the delay is in how work is handed off. We believe the tool addresses most of it, but that is an assumption.

**Your options**

| Option | Cost | Consequence |
|---|---|---|
| Do nothing | $0 up front | The gap with your competitor continues, and the errors keep costing you at an unmeasured rate. |
| **Quoting tool + 2 days training** | **$4,200 plus two days of staff time** | Targets both speed and accuracy. Staff time during training is a real cost, so schedule it in a slow week. |

**Recommendation:** Buy the tool and train the team. *Confidence: moderate-to-high.* The cost is small next to a single lost large job, and the downside of acting is limited. We would hold ourselves to a 60-day check: average quote time, and the share of quotes needing correction. If the numbers haven't moved, we revisit the process, not just the software.

**Decision needed:** Approve the $4,200 purchase and training by **November 20** *(proposed date)*. Each week of delay is another week of 3-day quotes against a same-day competitor.

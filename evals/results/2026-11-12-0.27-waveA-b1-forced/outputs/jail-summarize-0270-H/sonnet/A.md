# Quoting Decision Brief

**Decision needed:** Do you buy a $4,200 quoting tool and give your team two days of training?

## The answer
Yes. We recommend you buy it. Your quotes are slow, many contain mistakes, and your top competitor now quotes the same day. *(Judgment)*

## What we found
- Quotes take **3 days** on average. *(Fact, from our audit)*
- **4 in 10 quotes** have a pricing error that is caught later. *(Fact, from our audit)*
- Your top competitor quotes **the same day**. *(Fact, from our audit)*

A customer who gets a price today from a rival may not wait three days for yours. *(Inference)* When an error is caught later, you either absorb the cost or go back and ask the customer for more money. Both hurt your profit or your reputation. *(Inference)*

## What we don't know yet
- How many jobs you lost because quotes were slow, and what the errors cost in dollars. *(Unknown)*
- Whether the $4,200 is a one-time price or a yearly fee. We will confirm this with the seller. *(Unknown)*
- What two days of training costs you in lost work time. *(Unknown)*
- Whether the tool gets you to same-day quotes. We expect a big gain but have not tested it in your shop. *(Estimate)*

## Risks
- **Training pulls people off the floor for two days.** Jobs could slip. Schedule it in a slow week, or split it into shorter sessions.
- **The tool is only as good as the prices you feed it.** Wrong price lists in means wrong quotes out. Clean up the price list first.
- **Waiting has a cost too.** Every week you wait, you keep the 3-day delay and the errors.

## Your options
| Option | Cost | What you get |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Buy the tool and train the team** | $4,200 plus 2 days of staff time | Faster, checked quotes. Best chance of matching same-day. |
| 2. Fix it by hand (price checklist plus a second person who reviews each quote) | Staff time only, no cash | Fewer errors. Unlikely to reach same-day. *(Judgment)* |
| 3. Do nothing | $0 | The delays and errors continue while your rival keeps winning on speed. |

## Our recommendation
**Choose Option 1.** Confidence: medium. The problems are measured and clear. The dollar payoff is not yet measured. *(Judgment)*

**Decide by Friday, Nov 20, 2026** (our proposed date). Each week of delay means another week of 3-day quotes and about 4 in 10 with errors.

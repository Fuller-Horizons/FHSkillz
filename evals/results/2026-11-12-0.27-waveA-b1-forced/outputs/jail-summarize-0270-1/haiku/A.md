# Security Gaps: What to Fund This Month

**The answer:** Approve fixing all five security gaps by November 30, 2026. Two of them, the former staff email accounts and the shared password, can be closed this week for little cost. The other three need a quote before we can price them.

**Facts the decision rests on**

- A security review found five gaps. [User-provided]
- The device that lets staff connect from outside the office (called a VPN) runs old software with a serious, known flaw. [User-provided] We do not know whether anyone is exploiting it. [Unknown]
- The accounting system, QuickBooks Online, opens with a password alone. It has no second sign-in step. [User-provided]
- The whole office signs in to one shared administrator account. [User-provided]
- Two former employees still have working email accounts. [User-provided]
- Backups have not been test-restored in 14 months. [User-provided] We do not know whether they would restore. [Unknown]
- Fixing the accounts and passwords is mostly staff time. We estimate a few hours to two days. [Estimate]
- We have no price yet for the VPN fix or the backup test. [Unknown]

**Assumptions and unknowns**

We assume the review findings are accurate and current. We do not know whether the VPN flaw has been used against us. We do not know if the backups would work. We do not have a quote for the VPN work.

**Risks and what they could cost**

- **Former staff email.** Former staff can still read company messages and could send messages as the company. This could leak client information.
- **Shared password.** No one can be held responsible for changes. One leaked password opens everything.
- **No second sign-in step on accounting.** A stolen password could move money or change the books, with no extra check.
- **VPN flaw.** This is a known way into the office network. An attacker could reach every system, or lock files for ransom.
- **Untested backups.** If a serious failure or ransom attack happens, recovery may fail. That could mean weeks of lost work.

**Options**

- **A. Fix all five by November 30. Recommended.** Cost: a few days of staff time, plus the VPN quote (not yet known). Result: every gap is closed this month.
- **B. Fix the accounts now. Delay the VPN fix and backup test to next quarter.** Cost: lowest this month, a few days of staff time. Risk: the VPN flaw and the unknown backups stay open about three more months.
- **C. Defer everything.** Cost: nothing this month. Risk: every gap stays open. One incident would likely cost far more than all five fixes together.

**Recommendation (Judgment):** Choose Option A. We are highly confident about the account fixes. We are less certain about total cost until we have the VPN quote.

**Decision deadline:** Approve by November 20, 2026, so the work finishes by November 30. Each week the shared password and the VPN flaw stay open adds risk. The account fixes cost very little to do now.

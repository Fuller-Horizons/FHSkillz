**Audience: Board / CEO (one page).**

**Approve all five security fixes this month. Three of them are staff tasks that should start this week whether or not the budget is approved.**

**What the review found (facts from the review).** Five gaps need closing:

1. **The remote-access device (VPN) runs old software with a known critical flaw.** The vendor has published a fix, and this kind of flaw is actively exploited. *What it means (our assessment):* it is a known, open way into the whole office network. The fix is a vendor update, roughly a day of work (our estimate).
2. **The accounting system (QuickBooks Online) can be opened with a password alone.** *What it means:* a stolen password could allow altered financial records or redirected payments.
3. **Backups have not been test-restored in 14 months.** We believe backups run, but we have not shown we can recover from them. *What it means:* after a ransomware attack or hardware failure, recovery could take days or fail. This stays unverified until a test restore is done.
4. **The whole office shares one admin password.** *What it means:* no one can be held accountable for changes, and anyone who leaves keeps the keys.
5. **Two former employees still have active email accounts.** *What it means:* people no longer with us can read client and financial email, or send email as the company.

**Assumptions and unknowns.** Costs are not yet quoted. The VPN and backup-test effort figures are our estimates and need confirming with whoever supports IT. The review did not check whether any of these gaps has already been misused. That would need a separate look.

**Options.**

- **A. Fund all five this month (recommended).** Items 2, 4 and 5 are mostly staff time and should not wait for budget. The VPN update and backup test likely need outside IT help, so written quotes are needed first.
- **B. Do the quick fixes now and defer the VPN update and backup test to next month.** This saves this month's spend on those two items but leaves the known critical flaw open for roughly four to six more weeks (our estimate).
- **C. Defer everything.** This is the lowest spend now, but all five exposures stay open. Our judgment, not a priced figure: the cost of an incident (recovery, lost payments, client notification) would likely exceed the cost of option A. We have not priced that.

**Recommendation: Option A.** Confidence is high on direction, since these are standard, well-understood fixes. Confidence is medium on cost, since no quotes exist yet. If the quotes exceed budget, do the VPN update first and move the backup test to next month. Do not reverse that order.

**What happened on October 14, and what we changed**

On October 14, a misconfigured company file share made a spreadsheet of customer personal information viewable by anyone who had its link. The share stayed open for about six days and was locked down on October 20.

What we changed:
- IT has turned on link expiry for all file shares.
- IT will audit file shares every quarter.

What you can do:
- Before you share a file or link, check who can see it and whether it should expire.
- If you find a share or link that you did not expect to be open, tell IT right away.

# Newsletter item (draft, ready for Legal review)

**Audience row used:** non-technical all-staff, handled as the "owner/client" row (plain words, grade 9 or lower). It goes to part-timers and contractors, so it uses the widest-audience redaction.

**What we're telling you about a recent security slip**

Last month, a file we shared inside the company was set up the wrong way. For about a week, anyone who had the link could open it. The file held personal information about a small number of our customers. We fixed the problem as soon as we found it.

Here is what we know. We have no sign yet that anyone outside the company opened the file, but we are still checking.

Legal is reviewing the incident and will decide whether we must notify the customers affected. If you are asked about this by a customer or anyone outside the company, please don't answer. Send the question to your manager or to Legal.

IT has made two changes:
- Every shared link now expires on its own.
- IT will review all shared files every quarter.

Here is what you can do:
- Share files with named people, not "anyone with the link."
- Keep customer details out of spreadsheets when you can.
- If you spot a file shared too widely, tell IT right away.

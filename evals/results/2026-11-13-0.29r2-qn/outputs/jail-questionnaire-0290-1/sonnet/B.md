Purpose: Get ready for our first diagnostic meeting · From: Jonathan, Signal Results Group · To: [Owner name], [Company]

## Context
Before we meet, we want to understand where your company is headed, where money may be slipping away, and how ready the team is for change. We have you at about 20 employees on Long Island. If anything we have is wrong, tell us in the last box. Your answers stay between you and us.

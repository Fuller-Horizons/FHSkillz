# Gotchas — jail-cpr

- **Update theater.** An agenda of round-robin updates produces nothing a
  document couldn't. Updates → pre-work; meeting time is for Results.
- **Untestable Results.** "Align on strategy" can't be checked at close.
  "Decide between options A/B and assign the owner" can.
- **Ownerless items.** Topics without a named owner drift. Every item, one
  name.
- **Time fiction.** Eight items, sixty minutes, no buffer. Verify the
  arithmetic; cut items, not corners.
- **Decision ambush.** A decision item whose inputs weren't in pre-work
  produces a deferral, not a decision. The verify step checks the pairing.

## COMMIT-specific
- **Unverifiable corroboration.** "She'd say so herself, unprompted" isn't a
  check anyone controls — rendering the document doesn't make it provable.
  Score against a cadence or log you actually tracked, not against a third
  party's unprompted agreement.
- **Undefined qualifiers.** "Proper location," "a working system,"
  "meaningful progress" — each needs its own one-line operational
  definition attached (what does "working" mean, checked how?), or it's not
  testable at close. Attach the definition or cut the word.
- **DESIGN machinery leaking into COMMIT.** Owners other than the person,
  time-boxed items, an agenda — none belong in a personal/period
  commitment contract. If one shows up, the lane has drifted; strip back to
  Context · Purpose · Results.
- **Fuzzy deadline.** "By the end of the year" or "in a few months" is not a
  Deadline — a specific calendar date is. Every Result's own date is
  measured against it, so a soft Deadline makes every Result underneath it
  soft too.
